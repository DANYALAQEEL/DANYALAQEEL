Trigonometric Identities
However, it is important to recognize that some properties of the real 
trigonometric functions are not satisfied by their complex counterparts. 
For example, |𝑠𝑖𝑛𝑥| ≤1 and |𝑐𝑜𝑠𝑥| ≤1 for all real 𝑥, but these inequalities, 
in general, are not satisfied for complex input. Thus, we conclude that unlike 
the real sine and cosine functions, the complex sine and cosine functions are 
no longer bounded.
