# Elsa Energy - Smart Energy Management & Automation

## About Elsa Energy
Elsa Energy is a leading provider of intelligent living and energy management solutions in Pakistan. The company is dedicated to creating adaptable, energy-efficient living and working spaces through cutting-edge technology, automation, and real-time energy monitoring. Elsa's primary mission is to optimize energy consumption, reduce electricity bills, and enhance overall electrical safety.

## Core Smart Living Solutions

### 1. Smart Geyser Control
*   **Description:** Automates gas and electric water heaters (geysers).
*   **Features:** Remote temperature adjustment via the mobile app, customized weekly schedules, automatic shut-offs when optimal temperature is reached, and energy-saving modes.
*   **Benefits:** Prevents continuous energy usage, reduces gas and electricity bills by up to 30%, and prevents accidents due to overheating.

### 2. Smart Water Pump Automation
*   **Description:** Automates water tank levels and pump schedules.
*   **Features:** Sensors detect water levels in overhead and underground tanks, automatically starts/stops the pump, schedules pump operation during low tariff rates, and sends alerts for dry runs or pipe leaks.
*   **Benefits:** Prevents water overflow/wastage, protects pumps from dry-run damage, and saves electricity.

### 3. Appliance Control & Load Management
*   **Description:** Control high-load appliances like air conditioners, heaters, and washing machines.
*   **Features:** Real-time power usage monitoring (current, voltage, power factor), scheduling high-load appliance operations during off-peak hours, and automatic overload protection.

## Hardware Products (EMS Gateways)
Elsa Energy utilizes robust industrial-grade communication gateways for real-time data collection and device control:

### 1. USR-N510 (Serial to Ethernet Server)
*   **Role:** Acts as a gateway between Modbus RTU serial devices (like energy meters) and the cloud via Ethernet.
*   **Key Features:** High-speed transmission, electrostatic protection, industrial design, and support for Modbus gateway functions.

### 2. USR-M100 (Edge Computer / Gateway)
*   **Role:** An intelligent IoT edge gateway supporting multiple protocols (Modbus, MQTT, JSON).
*   **Key Features:** Edge computing capabilities, local data logging, digital/analog input-output pins, and cellular (4G/LTE) or Ethernet connectivity.

### 3. USR-M300 (Advanced Cloud Gateway)
*   **Role:** High-performance multi-port gateway for large residential or commercial installations.
*   **Key Features:** Dual Ethernet ports, robust routing, VPN security, edge script execution, and extensive protocol conversion.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q: How does Elsa EMS compare to installing Solar Panels?
*   **Answer:** Solar panels generate energy but do not manage how it is consumed. Elsa EMS focuses on consumption optimization, scheduling, and load control. EMS has a 5x lower upfront cost than solar, works regardless of weather conditions, actively predicts load faults, and helps you maximize your solar investment by scheduling heavy loads to run when solar generation is at its peak.

### Q: Can I control my devices when I am away from home?
*   **Answer:** Yes, all Elsa smart automation controllers connect to the cloud via Wi-Fi, Ethernet, or 4G. You can monitor and control all connected appliances using the Elsa mobile application from anywhere in the world.

### Q: What is an Energy Audit?
*   **Answer:** Elsa's professionals conduct an on-site or virtual energy audit, diagnostic checks on your heavy loads (ACs, pumps), and evaluate load balancing. The Elsa AI tool generates an electrical health scorecard identifying which appliances are drawing excess current or running inefficiently.
