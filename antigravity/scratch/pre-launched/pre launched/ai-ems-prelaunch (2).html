Frequently Noted Details

What occurs inside the Drag-and-Drop Timeline?

The timeline lets administrators slide heavy flexible appliances directly into periods with lower consumption averages or cheaper slab boundaries. Expected bill calculations adjust layout summaries instantly.

How do AI, Hybrid, and Manual control modes differ?

AI Mode automates shifts entirely; Hybrid breaks devices into Critical and Flexible lines where operators must confirm alterations manually; Manual treats AI choices as references only.

What is the role of the Slab Rate Tracker?

It acts as a continuous reference monitor. By calculating what rate tier your current aggregate system usage falls under, it handles automation sequencing to actively prevent sudden peak threshold jumps.

Is there role protection on these configurations?

Yes. Role-Based Access Control ensures that only designated profiles can manipulate timeline configurations or adjust automation parameters, while mobile companion layouts provide view-only trackers.
